AskMyDoc

Ask questions about your PDF and text files

Upload a PDF or TXT file and chat with it. Answers come from the retrieved passages of your document, and the model is instructed to say so when it finds no supporting text.

Open the app

How it works

  • Documents are split into parent and child chunks. Child chunks are embedded with all-MiniLM-L6-v2 and stored in ChromaDB.
  • The best-matching chunks are passed to an LLM: Ollama when running locally, Groq as the hosted option.
  • After each document-based answer, the app automatically runs an LLM-as-a-judge check that scores how many of the answer's claims the retrieved text supports. The score can be wrong or unavailable.

Limits

  • Supported file types: PDF and TXT. Up to 3 documents at a time in the UI.
  • Scanned PDFs fall back to the OCR.space API, which sends the file to that third-party service.
  • Answers can still be wrong. Check important facts against the source.
